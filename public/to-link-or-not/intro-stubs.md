# Block Introduction: Link Stubs

In this block, connections are shown as **short stubs** — partial lines extending a short distance from each node toward its neighbors, but not reaching them.

**Feedback note:** training tasks provide feedback after you submit. Real study trials in this block do **not** provide correctness feedback; after submitting, click **Next** to continue.

## What You Will See

- Every node has short line segments pointing outward in the direction of each of its connections.
- The stubs do not meet in the middle, so the full path of a connection is not drawn.
- The **number of stubs** on a node indicates how many connections it has.
- The **direction** of stubs indicates which nodes it connects to.

## How to Interact

- **Hover** over a node to highlight it.
- **Click** a node to select it as part of your answer.
- Selected nodes turn **green**.
- When you are satisfied with your selection, click **Submit Answer**.

## Tips

- For T1, count the number of stubs on each node — more stubs means more connections.
- For T2, look for nodes whose stubs point toward both orange-highlighted nodes.
- For T3, look for groups of nodes whose stubs point densely toward each other.
- Stubs give directional hints without the visual clutter of full crossing lines.
