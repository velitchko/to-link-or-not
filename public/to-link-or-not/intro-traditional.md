# Block Introduction: Traditional Links

In this block, connections between nodes are drawn as **solid lines**.

**Feedback note:** training tasks provide feedback after you submit. Real study trials in this block do **not** provide correctness feedback; after submitting, click **Next** to continue.

## What You Will See

- Every connection in the network is visible at all times as a line between two nodes.
- Thicker, denser regions indicate areas with more connections.

## How to Interact

- **Hover** over a node to highlight it (the node circle glows yellow).
- **Click** a node to select it as part of your answer.
- Selected nodes turn **green**.
- When you are satisfied with your selection, click **Submit Answer**.

## Tips

- In dense graphs, tracing individual lines can be tricky — follow them from endpoint to endpoint.
- For T1 (hub identification), count or estimate how many lines meet at each node.
- For T2 (common neighbors), look for nodes connected to both orange-highlighted nodes.
- For T3 (community detection), look for groups of nodes with more connections inside the group than outside.
