# Block Introduction: On-Demand Links

In this block, connections are **hidden by default** and revealed only when you hover over a node.

## What You Will See

- Nodes are displayed as colored circles with labels.
- No lines are visible initially.
- When you **hover** over a node, all edges connected to that node appear instantly.
- Moving away from a node hides its edges again.

## How to Interact

- **Hover** over nodes one at a time to explore their connections.
- **Click** a node to select it as part of your answer.
- Selected nodes turn **green**.
- When you are satisfied with your selection, click **Submit Answer**.

## Tips

- Hover over candidate nodes to count how many edges appear — this tells you the degree of that node.
- For T1, compare edge counts across nodes by hovering each one.
- For T2, hover each candidate node and check whether it shows edges to both orange nodes.
- For T3, hover nodes within a suspected cluster to confirm internal connectivity.

Take your time — you can hover as many nodes as you like before submitting.
