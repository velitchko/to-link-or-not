# Informed Consent

**Study Title:** To Link or Not — Evaluating Link-Rendering Strategies in Node-Link Diagrams

**Principal Investigator:** Velitchko Filipov, TU Wien

## Purpose

You are invited to participate in a research study examining how different visual representations of connections in network diagrams affect your ability to answer questions about the network. This study will take approximately **30–45 minutes**.

## What You Will Do

You will view a series of network diagrams and answer questions about each one. The study has **36 trials** grouped into four blocks. In each block you will use a different way of showing connections between nodes. Before the study trials, you will complete a short training section so you can learn the interactions.

The questions fall into three categories:

1. **Identifying the most-connected node** in the network
2. **Finding common neighbors** shared between two highlighted nodes
3. **Identifying clusters or communities** of nodes

During each trial, you will click nodes to select your answer and then submit it. You will receive feedback during training, but not during the real study trials. After each block, you will answer a short strategy question and a workload questionnaire about that block.

## Participation

- Participation is entirely **voluntary**. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.
- There are no known risks beyond those of normal computer use.
- Your responses will be recorded anonymously and used only for research purposes.
- De-identified data may be shared in academic publications or archived in a public repository.

## Data and Privacy

- No personally identifiable information is collected beyond what you provide here.
- Response data (answers, response times) is stored securely and accessible only to the research team.
- Data will be retained for a minimum of five years in accordance with institutional policy.
